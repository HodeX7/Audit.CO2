## 🎙️ Word-for-Word Script (Read Calmly & Confidently)

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] Act I: The Problem & The Solution
**Screen:** Show [`https://hodex7.github.io/Audit.CO2/`](https://hodex7.github.io/Audit.CO2/) (Full architecture overview).

> *"Corporate carbon accounting takes weeks of manual spreadsheet chaos across siloed teams. And when companies try generic AI chatbots, models hallucinate emissions data, risking millions in greenwashing fines.*  
>  
> *This is **Audit.CO2**—an autonomous multi-agent carbon accounting system powered by **Switch**."*

---

### [0:25 – 0:50] Act II: Why Switch is Indispensable
**Screen:** Hover over **Layer 1 (Switch Protocol)** and **Layer 2 (Specialized Agents)**.

> *"Why is Switch central here? Switch is not just a chat UI; it is our **authoritative governance boundary**.*  
>  
> *Our CLI intentionally has **zero** publish or approve commands. Every document upload, factor approval, and publication requires a verified Switch message ID and human role.*  
>  
> ***Removing Switch kills the application*** *— because without Switch, the chain of custody breaks and the system refuses to publish."*

---

### [0:50 – 1:40] Act III: The 4 Agents & The Architecture
**Screen:** Click smoothly through tabs **1 to 6** on the HTML page as you speak.

> *(Click Tab 1 & 2)*  
> *"Our architecture connects four specialized agents over duplex WebSockets.*  
>  
> *First, **`@carbon-room`** orchestrates the monthly close and locks regulatory factors.*  
> *Next, **`@collector`** ingests invoices using a **Zero-Hallucination Proof Boundary** that rejects any claim not matching the raw file bytes verbatim.*  
>  
> *(Click Tab 3 & 4)*  
> *Then, **`@watchdog`** evaluates deterministic greenhouse gas rules, catches compliance traps, and opens dedicated resolution threads in Switch.*  
> *Humans resolve these issues directly in threads—uploading missing bills and approving overrides—triggering real-time ledger recalculations.*  
>  
> *(Click Tab 5 & 6)*  
> *Finally, **`@reporter`** attaches mandatory regulatory disclosures and verifies the tamper-evident cryptographic audit chain in SQLite."*

---

### [1:40 – 2:15] Act IV: Live Mattermost Tour
**Screen:** Switch tab to Mattermost `#emissions-reporting` channel. Scroll smoothly.

> *"Here in our Switch Mattermost channel, you can see this live.*  
>  
> *The Lead starts the close, and `@carbon-room` coordinates the plan.*  
> *`@watchdog` identifies compliance gaps and spins out targeted threads.*  
>  
> *(Click open a thread like Gap 3 or Gap 1)*  
> *Inside the threads, our facility manager uploads the missing bill, and the Lead resolves invoice conflicts—keeping the main channel completely clean.*  
>  
> *(Scroll to the bottom)*  
> *At the bottom, `@reporter` drafts the disclosure, `@carbon-room` publishes the signed reports, and anyone can verify the cryptographic audit chain on demand."*

### [2:15 – 2:30] Act V: Winning Conclusion
**Screen:** Switch back to the Architecture Visualizer.

> *Switch makes this possible by providing the trusted communication bus and governance boundary enterprise compliance demands.*  
>  
> *Flip the Switch on carbon accounting. Thank you!"*
